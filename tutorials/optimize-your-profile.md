# Optimize Your Profile

A strong LinkedIn profile does not need to sound impressive at every line. It needs to be coherent.

Start by reviewing the profile as a whole. The photo, headline, summary, experience, featured items, and skills should reinforce one another. If each section presents a different version of you, the profile becomes harder to trust.

Your headline should explain more than your current title. It should help a stranger understand your focus. In many cases, a good headline combines role, domain, and a small amount of specificity.

The About section should help the reader understand your work, perspective, or priorities. It does not need to sound like a speech. Clear language is stronger than inflated language.

Finally, review the profile as a system. Ask whether the profile supports the opportunities you want now, not the version of yourself you needed to describe several years ago.
