# Publish a LinkedIn Newsletter

A LinkedIn newsletter should feel like a recurring editorial product, not a series of disconnected posts.

Begin with a clear editorial promise. The reader should understand what the newsletter covers and why it is worth subscribing to. Consistency matters more than novelty.

Create a repeatable issue structure. This helps readers know what to expect and makes production easier over time. A stable structure also improves your ability to compare issues and refine your approach.

When adapting material from your website or another publication, edit for the LinkedIn context. Readers on LinkedIn often need tighter framing and faster orientation.

The goal is not only to publish issues, but to build a recognizable and reliable series.
