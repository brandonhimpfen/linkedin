# Personal Profile Checklist

Use this checklist when creating or improving a personal LinkedIn profile.

## Identity and clarity

- [ ] Profile photo is current, clear, and professional.
- [ ] Headline explains role, focus, or value clearly.
- [ ] Location and industry details are accurate where relevant.
- [ ] Public-facing profile language matches current positioning.

## About section

- [ ] Summary explains who you are and what you do.
- [ ] Summary reflects current work rather than outdated roles.
- [ ] Tone is clear and human rather than inflated.
- [ ] Contact or website links are current where appropriate.

## Experience and proof

- [ ] Experience entries are updated.
- [ ] Role descriptions explain substance, not just titles.
- [ ] Featured section includes useful examples of work.
- [ ] Skills and endorsements are curated and not cluttered.

## Visibility and consistency

- [ ] Profile visuals align with your broader web presence.
- [ ] Links and featured items are working.
- [ ] Grammar and punctuation have been reviewed.
- [ ] Profile feels coherent when scanned top to bottom.
