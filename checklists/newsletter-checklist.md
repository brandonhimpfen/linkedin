# Newsletter Checklist

Use this checklist when preparing a LinkedIn newsletter issue.

## Editorial planning

- [ ] The issue has a clear theme or focus.
- [ ] The title is specific and consistent with the newsletter identity.
- [ ] The introduction sets expectations quickly.

## Reader experience

- [ ] The issue is easy to scan.
- [ ] Sections are balanced and not overloaded.
- [ ] Links and references are relevant.
- [ ] The tone fits the newsletter's audience.

## Operational review

- [ ] Formatting has been checked in preview.
- [ ] Reused content has been adapted rather than pasted carelessly.
- [ ] The issue feels additive, not repetitive.
- [ ] Publish timing supports the newsletter cadence.
