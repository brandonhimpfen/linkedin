# Article Checklist

Use this checklist when publishing a long-form LinkedIn article.

## Editorial structure

- [ ] Title is clear and specific.
- [ ] Introduction explains the article's purpose quickly.
- [ ] Headings guide the reader logically.
- [ ] The article has a coherent through-line.

## Substance

- [ ] The article provides actual insight, not just summary.
- [ ] Examples are relevant and useful.
- [ ] Claims are not exaggerated.
- [ ] The conclusion reinforces the main point without repeating too much.

## Publishing review

- [ ] Formatting is clean.
- [ ] Visuals are placed intentionally.
- [ ] Links work correctly.
- [ ] The article reflects your current thinking and standards.
