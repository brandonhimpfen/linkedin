# Text Post Template

Use this as a practical starting point, then rewrite it into your own voice.

## Template

Opening line that introduces the topic clearly.

A short paragraph explaining why the topic matters.

A second paragraph that shares the core insight, observation, or example.

A final paragraph that closes the loop and, if appropriate, invites thoughtful discussion.

## Notes

- Keep the post focused on one main idea
- Avoid adding a call to action just because it seems expected
- Prefer clarity to performance tactics
