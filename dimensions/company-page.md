# Company Page Dimensions

This file covers practical guidance for LinkedIn company page visuals.

## Logo

Use a clean square mark that remains identifiable at small sizes.

Practical guidance:

- Aspect ratio: 1:1.
- Prefer a simple mark or high-contrast wordmark treatment.
- Avoid fine details that disappear in small renderings.

## Cover image

Use a wide banner image designed with generous safe areas.

Practical guidance:

- Landscape orientation
- Keep key visual elements toward the center.
- Avoid putting essential text too close to the edges.

## Design notes

- Use branding that is consistent with your website and other channels.
- Keep banners current if your positioning changes.
- Avoid cluttered hero-style visuals that reduce readability.
