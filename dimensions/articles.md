# Article Visual Guidance

Long-form LinkedIn articles benefit from simple, clear supporting visuals rather than overly promotional assets.

## Header image

Use a wide image that reflects the topic and remains readable when cropped.

## Inline visuals

Use diagrams, screenshots, and charts only when they genuinely improve understanding.

## Practical notes

- Choose clarity over decoration.
- Keep styles consistent across related articles.
- Avoid using too many image styles in one article.
